# `eu-2025-105`: Decision 2006/771/EC, annex as replaced by (EU) 2025/105

- **Act:** Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/105 of 22 January 2025
  amending Decision 2006/771/EC (OJ L series 2025/105, 23.1.2025), CELEX
  `32025D0105`. Its Article 1(3): "the Annex is replaced by the text set out
  in the Annex to this Decision."
- **URL:** <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2025/105/oj>; retrieved
  as XHTML from <https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32025D0105>
  (`Accept: application/xhtml+xml`), because EUR-Lex refuses scripted
  downloads.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-20.
- **Later changes:** the Publications Office metadata of 2006/771/EC
  (`resource/celex/32006D0771`, retrieved 2026-11-20) lists no amending act
  after (EU) 2025/105. The tables before this check cited (EU) 2022/180,
  whose annex 2025/105 replaced; the numbers of the rows used here are the
  same in both (44a lost its −13 dBm/10 kHz clause, which only applied above
  250 kHz bandwidth; 45c now says "bandwidth ≤ 25 kHz" instead of "channel
  spacing up to 25 kHz").
- **Binding force:** the annex, "General technical conditions": "Member States
  shall allow the usage of spectrum up to the transmit power, field strength
  or power density set out in Table 2." The `EU` table, and through `Base: EU`
  every member state's table, uses these rows.

Conversion (Q.2): dBm = 10·log10(mW), rounded down, capped at +10:
1 mW → 0, 5 mW → 6.99 → **6**, 10 mW → 10, 25 mW → 13.98 → 10,
500 mW → 26.99 → 10. The duty-cycle alternative is used because the
"techniques to access spectrum and mitigate interference" (LBT + AFA) are not
implemented.

Not used (their categories are not "non-specific" or need a network role):
46b (audio PMSE), 47a (RFID interrogators), 47b and 84 (data networks [g]),
49, 51, 52, 53, 55 ("Reliable alarm devices").
