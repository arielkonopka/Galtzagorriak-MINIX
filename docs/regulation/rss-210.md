## Annex A.1: momentarily operated devices (`ctl`, `per`)

- A.1.1: "The frequency bands and field strength limits in table A1 and table A2
  are reserved exclusively for the transmission of a control signal, such as
  those used with alarm systems, door openers, remote switches, etc. Data may
  be sent with a control signal." "prohibited in the restricted frequency
  bands listed in RSS-Gen".
- A.1.2: "When released, the transmitter shall cease transmission within no
  more than 5 seconds of being released. A transmitter that has been activated
  automatically shall cease transmission within 5 seconds of activation.
  Periodic transmissions at regular, predetermined intervals are not allowed,
  except as specified in section A.1.5." → `ctl`.
- A.1.3, Table A1: "260-470** | 3,750 to 12,500*"; "for 260-470 MHz: field
  strength (µV/m) = (41.6667 x f) - 7083.3333". Footnote **: "Frequency bands
  225-328.6 MHz and 335.4-399.9 MHz are designated for use by the Government
  of Canada. Manufacturers should be aware of possible harmful interference and
  degradation of their licence-exempt radio equipment in these frequency
  bands."
- A.1.4: "The occupied bandwidth of momentarily operated devices shall be less
  than or equal to 0.25% of the centre frequency for devices operating between
  70 MHz and 900 MHz."
- A.1.5: "Devices may be employed for any type of operation provided they
  comply with the requirements of section A.1.4 and with the field strength
  limits specified in table A2. ... the duration of each transmission is not
  greater than 1 second and the silent period between transmissions is at
  least 30 times the duration of the transmission, but not less than 10 seconds
  under any circumstances." Table A2: "260-470** | 1,500 to 5,000*"; "for
  260-470 MHz: field strength (µV/m) = (16.6667 x f) - 2833.3333". → `per`.

These formulas give the same field strengths as 47 CFR 15.231(b)/(e) (to
0.01 µV/m), so the `ctl` and `per` rows, their dBm, bandwidths and split points
(308.565, 416.406 MHz) are those worked out in [cfr-47-15.md](cfr-47-15.md).

## Annex B.10: 902–928 MHz (`any`)

"Devices operating in the frequency bands listed in table B2 may be used for
any application". Table B2: "902-928 | 50" mV/m at 3 m (fundamental), "except
for the fundamental emission in the frequency band 902-928 MHz, which is based
on measurements using a CISPR quasi-peak detector." → −1.25 dBm → `-2`.

## RSS-Gen 7.5, Table 6: restricted bands

"The occupied bandwidth of licence-exempt radio apparatus shall be entirely
outside the restricted bands and the prohibited TV bands of 54-72 MHz,
76-88 MHz, 174-216 MHz and 470-602 MHz." Table 6 in the CC1101 ranges:
"322 to 335.4", "399.9 to 410" MHz (next: 240 to 285, 608 to 614). No row of
`CA.reg` overlaps them.
