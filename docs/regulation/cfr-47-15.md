## 15.205(a): restricted bands

The list, in the CC1101 ranges (300–348, 387–464, 779–928 MHz), contains
"322-335.4" and "399.9-410" MHz; the next ones are 240-285 and 608-614 MHz.
No row of `US.reg` overlaps them.

## 15.231: `ctl` and `per` rows

- (a): "the intentional radiator is restricted to the transmission of a control
  signal such as those used with alarm systems, door openers, remote switches,
  etc. ... Data is permitted to be sent with a control signal." (a)(1) "within
  not more than 5 seconds of being released", (a)(2) "shall cease transmission
  within 5 seconds after activation", (a)(3) "Periodic transmissions at regular
  predetermined intervals are not permitted." → `use` `ctl`.
- (b), table: "260-470 | 3,750 to 12,500" µV/m, footnote "Linear
  interpolations"; (b)(1) "specified at a distance of 3 meters. The tighter
  limits apply at the band edges."
- (c): "The bandwidth of the emission shall be no wider than 0.25% of the
  center frequency for devices operating above 70 MHz and below 900 MHz."
- (e): "may be employed for any type of operation ... provided the intentional
  radiator complies with the provisions of paragraphs (b) through (d) ...
  except the field strength table ... is replaced by": "260-470 | 1,500 to
  5,000" µV/m; "the duration of each transmission shall not be greater than one
  second and the silent period between transmissions shall be at least 30
  times the duration of the transmission but in no case less than 10 seconds."
  → `use` `per`.

Each row uses the field strength at its lower edge (the lowest in the row),
E(f) = 3750 + 8750·(f − 260)/210 µV/m for (b), 1500 + 3500·(f − 260)/210 for
(e), e.i.r.p. = (E · 3 m)² / 30 Ω, rounded down; bandwidth = 0.25 % of the
lower edge, rounded down.

## 15.247: the `fhss` row

- (a)(1)(i): "if the 20 dB bandwidth of the hopping channel is less than
  250 kHz, the system shall use at least 50 hopping frequencies and the average
  time of occupancy on any frequency shall not be greater than 0.4 seconds
  within a 20 second period".
- (b)(2): "For frequency hopping systems operating in the 902-928 MHz band:
  1 watt for systems employing at least 50 hopping channels".
- → `902.000 928.000 10 none 250 fhss` (+30 dBm capped at +10).

## 15.249(a): the 902–928 MHz `any` row

- "902-928 MHz | 50" millivolts/meter (fundamental); (c) "Field strength
  limits are specified at a distance of 3 meters."
- (0.05 V/m · 3 m)² / 30 Ω = 0.75 mW = −1.25 dBm → `-2`.
