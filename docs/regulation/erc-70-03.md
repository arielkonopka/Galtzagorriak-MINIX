# `erc-70-03`: ERC Recommendation 70-03, edition of October 2026

- **Document:** ERC Recommendation 70-03 "Relating to the use of Short Range
  Devices (SRD)", approved 1997 (Tromsø), "Subsequent amendments 9 October
  2026", "Edition of October 2026" (cover page; the Annex 1 pages carry the
  footer "Edition of October 2025", Appendix 1 and 2 "Edition of October 2026").
- **URL:** <https://docdb.cept.org/download/5013> (listed on
  <https://docdb.cept.org/document/845>), PDF, 87 pages.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-20.
- **Note:** spec Q.1/Q.4/Q.5 and the tables before this check used the
  March 2024 edition. Appendix 1 has changed since then for several countries
  (see "Changes from March 2024" below).

## Appendix 2 "National restrictions", PDF pages 51-55: the remarks that matter

- General (all annexes): Albania: "Frequencies covered by ERC/REC 70-03 are
  implemented through the notes of the National Frequency Table, for each band
  mentioned in 70-03". Norway: "The Regulations do not apply to frequencies in
  the range of 2 GH–32 GHz ... Ny-Ålesund at Svalbard" (outside the CC1101's
  ranges). No general remark for Bosnia and Herzegovina, North Macedonia or
  the United Kingdom.
- Band G1, G2, H1.x: remarks only for Portugal (G1: analogue audio/video
  excluded) and Ireland (G2, H1.8: voice rules; H1.0-H1.9: access techniques
  apply), none for AL, BA, MK, NO, GB.
- Band H2 (870-874.4): Belgium "870-873 MHz"; Finland "Limited to 870-873
  MHz"; Germany "not available"; Hungary "With ER-GSM protection"; Spain "See
  national note UN-40 in the NTAF"; Sweden "Usage are based on licenses ...";
  United Kingdom "The Additional restrictions to protect ER-GSM apply in the
  UK".
- Band H3 (915-919.4): Belgium "915-918MHz"; Czech Republic "Implemented in
  the 915.2-919.4 MHz band. In the 915-915.2 MHz range, duty cycle is limited
  to 0.1%"; Romania "lmplementation limited to 917.4-919.4 MHz"; Sweden as
  for H2; United Kingdom as for H2.

## What this verifies

- **AL, BA, MK, NO, GB:** every row whose band is "Y" for the country, with
  no Appendix 2 restriction, is active and verified against Annex 1. Rows with
  "?" stay `#?` (unverified: no information); rows with "N" are left out.
  h2/h3 stay `#?` everywhere until the national text is read (Q.5).
- **BA** (rows taken only from the spec's matrix before): the 2026 statuses
  are exactly those of Q.4 (g1-g3, h1.5-h1.8 Y; h0, h1.3 ?; h1.4, h1.9, h2,
  h3 N). Verified as shipped.
- **MK**: all Annex 1 bands "Y" (h1.9 included). Verified as shipped; h2/h3
  stay `#?` (AEK text not read).
- **AL**: unchanged from Q.4 (h1.3, h1.4, h1.9 "?").
- **NO, GB** (corrected): h0 862-863 MHz is "Y" for both; it was `#?` only
  because the status had not been read. Now active.
- **Member states**, h2/h3 `#?` rows: status text updated to 2026. CZ ("?" →
  Y/L), HR ("U" → Y/Y): rows kept as `#?`. MT (now N/N) and RO h2 (now N):
  rows removed (Q.5: "N" is left out). Still `#?` everywhere.

## Changes from March 2024 (Q.4/Q.5 as written)

Q.5's lists of h2/h3 statuses were made from the March 2024 edition; the
statuses that now differ for the tables' countries: CZ h2 Y, h3 L; HR h2/h3 Y;
MT h2/h3 N; RO h2 N, h3 L; and for NO and GB the h0 to h3 statuses are now
read (all Y). The tables follow the October 2026 edition.
