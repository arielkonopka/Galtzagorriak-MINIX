wirefind - metal, mains, data cables
Needs no extra hardware.

METAL  (125 kHz coil)
 Hold away from the wall,
 press OK = baseline. Then
 bring the back of the
 Flipper close.
MAINS  (50 Hz, GPIO pin)
 Pin A7 (header pin 3) is
 the antenna. A bare wire
 in the pin helps; a 10 MOhm
 resistor from the pin to
 GND is better, not needed.
 Pitch = strength.
 Left/Right = sensitivity
 0-7: search far at 7, then
 narrow down. 10-20 cm in a
 wall is the aim.
DATA   (CC1101, experimental)
 Baseline away from the
 wall, as for metal.
Back = exit.
